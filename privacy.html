Privacy Policy — Haru Pinterest Connector

Effective: 27 September 2026
Operator: Haru (GitHub: hrusyd)
Contact: Open a privacy request on GitHub. Issues are public: do not post account secrets, private data, or credentials. You can request a private contact channel there without sharing sensitive details.

Purpose

This private-use integration is for the owner's Pinterest Business account. It is designed to read the owner's Pins and boards, retrieve publicly available Pins and boards from other accounts if Pinterest separately approves that access, and create Pins on the owner's account only at the owner's request. It does not seek nonpublic content from other accounts. Features requiring approval will not be used until approval is granted.

Information accessed

Subject to authorization, the integration may access the owner's Pinterest account identifier, board and Pin identifiers, names, descriptions, images, and source links. To create a Pin it may process the selected board, image URL, title, description, and destination URL. If separately permitted by Pinterest, it may retrieve public Pin and board metadata from other accounts when requested by the owner. It requests only permissions needed for enabled functions.

Use and sharing

This information is used to answer the owner's requests and perform actions they direct. Relevant request data may be processed by the AI model provider configured for the owner's Hermes Agent installation. The connector communicates with Pinterest's official API; hosting and infrastructure providers may process limited operational data. The operator does not sell Pinterest account data, use it for unrelated profiling, or offer access to other users' private accounts.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and app secrets, once issued, will be stored on the operator's private server and never published in this repository. The Hermes Agent installation may retain conversations, tool results, and logs until the owner deletes them. Revoking Pinterest authorization stops future authorized API access but does not by itself erase existing local logs. The owner may ask the operator to delete locally held credentials and related data, subject to necessary security or legal retention.

Security and choices

Credentials and locally stored data are restricted to the operator's private installation. No system guarantees absolute security. The owner can revoke the app's access in Pinterest account settings and request local data deletion using the contact above.

Changes

Material changes will be published on this page. The effective date will be updated when this policy changes.
