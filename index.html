Haru Pinterest Connector

Haru Pinterest Connector is a private-use integration for the owner's Pinterest Business account. The planned functions are viewing the owner's boards and Pins, discovering publicly available Pins and boards from other accounts if Pinterest approves that access, and creating Pins on the owner's account only at the owner's direction through a private Hermes Agent installation.

The app does not offer public sign-up or seek other users' nonpublic content. Access to public content from other accounts requires Pinterest approval and depends on official API capabilities. The app is not connected to Pinterest yet.
